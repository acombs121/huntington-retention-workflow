Part 1: The Challenge

The Business Problem: A Two-Sided Bottleneck & 1031 Flight

Huntington's commercial book experiences $4.5B in annual loan payoffs across its 1,400-branch, 21-state footprint as a top-2 national SBA 7(a) lender. Each payoff represents an urgent liquidity event. Today, manual bandwidth limitations across both commercial banking and wealth management create an unavoidable deposit leakage point.

The Problem in Practice

The Core Problem: Commercial Loan Payoffs Trigger Deposit Flight

The Problem When a commercial borrower sells a property and pays off their Huntington loan, millions of dollars in net cash are suddenly released. Because Huntington lacks early automated visibility into title payoff requests before closing, ~78% of these deposits wire out to competing institutions within 48 to 72 hours—costing the bank both the loan asset and the resulting deposit relationship.

The Example Commercial borrower Marcus Vance sells Riverfront Commons for $8.5M, triggering a $5.2M Huntington CRE loan payoff and releasing $2.9M in net cash. At closing, Marcus must decide between taking taxable cash or rolling into an IRC §1031 exchange—and in either case, without early intervention, those funds leave Huntington forever:

Tax-Deferred Roll & Replacement Financing
Commercial CRE Reality • Institutional Depository Protection
$2.9M Escrow Custody Client defers capital gains & depreciation recapture by acquiring replacement property
  • The IRS Rule: Under Treas. Reg. § 1.1031(k)-1(f), the client cannot touch the funds — constructive receipt voids the deferral. Huntington confines itself to the escrow-depository role and uses an independent Qualified Intermediary. That is a deliberate risk posture, not an established legal bar; tax counsel should confirm before it is offered.
  • The Flight Risk: Without an institutional custody integration, 100% of exchange proceeds legally wire out to external third-party exchange accommodators.
  • Book Scout Solution: Book Scout picks up exchange intent from what the borrower actually asks us for — a request to coordinate escrow, or a named intermediary — and routes to the Huntington 1031 Qualified Escrow Depository (partnered with IPX1031) under the qualified-escrow safe harbor at Treas. Reg. § 1.1031(k)-1(g)(3). The routine-financial-services carve-out at § 1.1031(k)-1(k)(2)(ii) is what permits the bank to hold that escrow. Firewalling in-house DST cross-sell during the window is a securities-conflict control we impose on ourselves, not a tax requirement.
  • Capacity Impact: Retains the $2.9M institutional deposit for 180 days, delivers 4.75% escrow yield, and pre-qualifies Huntington Commercial Lending to originate the replacement mortgage.

Side 1: Commercial RM Bottleneck

Commercial Discovery Drag

Commercial Relationship Managers (RMs) are focused on loan origination. They lack the bandwidth to manually hunt for exits across thousands of servicing tickets.

6 to 8 Hours / Deal
Manual time across 3 systems (core servicing, credit vault, CRM) to resolve LLC guarantors, calculate equity, and check tax intent.
  • Un-monitorable Book: Thousands of payoff requests arrive via PDF from title companies and sit unread in servicing queues.
  • Entity Resolution Gap: Borrower LLCs conceal living owners; parsing incumbency certificates is manual and labor-intensive.
  • The Title Wire Deadline: Title companies disburse funds based on seller wire instructions signed days before closing. Without early contact, the wire routes away.
Side 2: Wealth Advisor Bottleneck

Wealth Onboarding & Ongoing Servicing Drag

Private Wealth Advisors (PWAs) manage full client books (80–100 families). Simply sending them more commercial leads just moves the bottleneck without scaling the business.

80–100 Client Ceiling
Manual onboarding takes 2–3 weeks, and ongoing fiduciary servicing (quarterly reviews, asset allocation tracking) limits advisor bandwidth.
  • Capacity Throttle: Advisors cannot absorb new multi-million dollar relationships without ongoing fiduciary servicing fatigue.
  • The 1031 Exchange Blind Spot: Over 50–65% of commercial dispositions execute IRC §1031 exchanges. If funds touch operating accounts, tax deferral is voided.
  • Flight Window Lost: Proceeds wire out to Wall Street wirehouses or third-party exchange accommodators in 48–72 hours.

Autonomous Liquidity Detection, Enrichment & Routing

Huntington Book Scout introduces an intelligent agentic orchestration layer built on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (fka Vertex AI Platform). Specialized autonomous agents continually monitor the franchise book, resolve beneficial ownership, match private wealth advisors, and automate personalized relationship manager outreach.

Continuous Surveillance Signals

What the Agents Watch

Autonomous multi-source ingestion fusing commercial loan books, wire clearing, entity hierarchies, and public deed exhibits in real time.

Core Ledger • Commercial LOS

Loan Payoff & Early Paydown Events

Monitors payoff demands, scheduled maturities, and especially early paydown events on commercial real estate (CRE) facilities and term loans across all 1,400 branches.

Fedwire • Title Escrow

Large Inbound Settlement Wires

Detects substantial inbound wire disbursements credited to an LLC operating account originating from title insurance companies, commercial escrow agents, or M&A legal counsel.

Deposit Core • Liquidity Triage

Dormant Balance Spikes

Surfaces sudden, high-magnitude balance surges that sit idle in business checking accounts beyond calibrated balance thresholds and idle duration windows.

Entity Resolution • Core Master

Beneficial Ownership & Guarantors

Ingests operating agreements and corporate incumbency certificates to map complex pass-through LLC structures to individual human principals, primary guarantors, and existing personal accounts.

Public Records • Registries

External Confirmation Signals

Cross-validates transactions against external public registries including county deed transfers, UCC lien releases, and verified press on business sales and M&A transactions.

End-to-End Autonomous Execution

The Agent Workflow End to End

Five coordinated agents execute deterministic analysis, enrichment, routing, and post-closing tracking with human-in-the-loop governance.

01 Detect
Detection Agent Signal Fusion • Flight Risk Scoring

Fuses signals into a composite score for the liquidity event, distinguishing payoffs from refis and 1031s. The detection agent identifies a payoff with no proceeds, no replacement loan, and an identifiable intermediary which directly informs classification.

Key Output: Calibrated Liquidity Score (e.g., 94%) • Deterministic Classification: Refinance vs. 1031 vs. Taxable Cash-Out
02 Enrich
Enrichment Agent Dossier Synthesis • Capital Modeling

Builds an executive 1-pager briefing in seconds: relationship history, who the commercial banker is, what the proceeds likely are, tax implications, and personal wealth objectives, mapping the borrower's total Huntington footprint.

Key Output: Executive Relationship 1-Pager, Net Proceeds Sizing (~$2.90M) • Tax & Wealth Objectives Dossier
03 Route
Routing Agent Multi-Factor Matching • RM Notification

Selects the right advisor based on geography, capacity, and specialty, sending real-time notifications to align the commercial RM and private wealth advisor.

Key Output: Ranked Wealth Advisor Match (98% Match Score) • Automated Dual-Party RM Notification
04 Outreach
Outreach Agent Executive Drafting • Call Scheduling

Creates a warm intro for the RM to the advisor. The intro is sent on behalf of the commercial banker—they own the client relationship, ensuring they stay firmly in the loop—while pre-populating context and coordinating call scheduling.

Key Output: RM-Authored Introduction Draft, Pre-Populated Deal Context • Automated Intro Call Scheduling
05 Track
Tracking Agent Deposit Retention • AUM Conversion Telemetry

Watches the funds and stays on the money until it's either captured or deployed. Tracks cycle time from notification to outreach to conversation to conversion to verify advisors are successfully converting leads into assets under management (AUM).

Key Output: Deposit Velocity • Milestone Funnel Tracking: Notification → Outreach → Conversation → Conversion
