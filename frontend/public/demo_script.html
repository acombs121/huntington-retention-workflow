Huntington Horizon 2.0 Customer Demo Script

What Problem Does This Solve?

Huntington's commercial book generates $4.5B in annual commercial real estate (CRE), middle-market, and SBA 7(a) loan payoffs across 1,400 branches in 21 states. As a top-2 SBA 7(a) lender nationally, our book is packed with middle-market business sales and commercial property exits. Within 48–72 hours of closing, ~78% of net proceeds ($2M–$10M+) wire out to competitors because commercial exits take 6–9 months, title company payoff faxes sit in loan ops queues for days, and 1031 exchanges leak to outside accommodators. Horizon 2.0 deploys a Two-Tier Retention Shield: locking entity liquidity on Huntington's balance sheet on Day 0 via Business Premier Insured Cash Sweep (ICS) (4.85% APY) and Institutional 1031 Qualified Escrow Depository, and engaging personal wealth advisory post-distribution via cloud-native SEI Data Cloud integration under strict Ameriprise Regulation R Networking compliance.
