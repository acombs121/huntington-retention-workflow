What Problem Does This Solve?

Huntington's commercial book generates $4.5B in annual commercial real estate (CRE), middle-market, and SBA 7(a) loan payoffs across 1,400 branches in 21 states. As a top-2 SBA 7(a) lender nationally, our franchise is rich with middle-market business sales and commercial property exits. Within 48–72 hours of closing, ~78% of net proceeds ($2M–$10M+) routinely wire out to Wall Street wirehouses because commercial exits take 6–9 months, title company payoff faxes sit in loan ops queues for days, and 1031 exchanges leak to outside accommodators.

Horizon 2.0 deploys a Two-Tier Retention Shield: locking entity liquidity on Huntington's balance sheet on Day 0 via Business Premier Insured Cash Sweep (ICS) (4.85% APY) and Institutional 1031 Qualified Escrow Depository, and engaging personal wealth advisory post-distribution via cloud-native SEI Data Cloud integration under strict Ameriprise Regulation R Networking compliance.

Anticipated Executive Committee Q&A (Presenter Defense)
Q1 How does the Ameriprise partnership impact client data sharing?
Less than people assume, because of how the arrangement is actually structured. Huntington employs the advisors and the client stays a Huntington customer. Ameriprise provides the technology platform, clearing and back office, and acts as the supervising broker-dealer. So when Greg hands Marcus to Sarah Jenkins, that is an intra-institutional handoff between two Huntington employees — not a disclosure to a non-affiliated third party, and Reg P opt-out does not attach to it. Ameriprise does receive client NPI through the platform, governed as a service-provider relationship under 12 C.F.R. § 1016.13 — contractual confidentiality and use limitations rather than customer opt-out. The two controls that genuinely bind us are Regulation R, capping Greg's referral compensation at a nominal, non-contingent, fixed-dollar amount with zero securities fee-splitting; and Reg BI supervision, which sits with Ameriprise as the supervising broker-dealer — precisely why Horizon generates administrative scaffolding only and never a recommendation.
Q2 How does Horizon 2.0 connect to Huntington Private Bank's wealth platform?
We integrate natively with the SEI Wealth Platform (SWP) via the SEI Data Cloud (announced March 31, 2026). This utilizes Snowflake Secure Data Sharing (Zero-ETL) to securely exchange portfolio telemetry and verify account status in real time, completely bypassing legacy on-premises trust accounting batch files like Trust 3000.
Q3 Why does Huntington's top-2 SBA 7(a) lender status matter for this platform?
Huntington's commercial payoff book is not just suburban office buildings. Across 1,400 branches in 21 states, Huntington leads the nation in SBA 7(a) lending. SBA loan payoffs represent business sales, acquisitions, and entrepreneur retirements—inflection points where middle-market business owners experience multi-million-dollar liquidity windfalls. Horizon 2.0 monitors SBA 7(a) maturity queues and SOP 50 10 7 prepayment notices to capture entrepreneur wealth.
Q4 Why don't we deliver wire instructions directly to the title company?
Because lenders have zero legal standing to direct seller proceeds. Under ALTA Pillar 2 and UCC Article 4A, title companies disburse seller equity strictly pursuant to the Seller's Closing Disbursement Instructions executed by the seller. Delivering lender instructions directly to title creates wire fraud liability and is rejected out of hand. Horizon 2.0 generates a verified routing packet delivered to Marcus Vance via DocuSign, who submits it as his official seller instruction backed by our official bank verification letter.
Q5 What prevents Huntington from being disqualified on a 1031 exchange?
Horizon 2.0 strictly firewalls 1031 escrow from wealth management and Delaware Statutory Trust (DST) placement desks. Huntington National Bank acts solely as the institutional escrow depository under an independent, unaffiliated national Qualified Intermediary (IPX1031). By prohibiting in-house DST securities sales during the 180-day window, we remain firmly within the Treas. Reg. § 1.1031(k)-1(k)(2)(ii) routine banking safe harbor.
Q6 Why does it cost $1.25M annually to run this platform?
Raw AI compute is minimal ($25k/yr). The $1.25M fully-loaded budget funds enterprise Cloud Spanner, Apigee X API gateway integration, SEI Data Cloud connectivity, annual SOC2 and OCC SR 11-7 model validations, and a dedicated 2-person platform engineering and MLOps pod.
Q7 Our trust assets dropped 62% year over year. Why are we investing in wealth?
Because that number measures the business you left, not the business you are in. Q2 2026 10-Q Table 24 shows total trust assets of $68.9B against $182.8B a year prior. Call Report Schedule RC-T decomposes it exactly: custody and safekeeping ran off $101.9B and corporate trust exited entirely — 5,565 accounts to 3. Over the same twelve months managed fiduciary assets grew 41% to $39.8B and managed accounts grew 29% to 23,481. The decisive figure is income: trust assets fell 62% while gross fiduciary fee income rose 17%, $114.0M to $133.3M. What left earned 0.78 bps (custody) and 6.15 bps (corporate trust); the managed advisory book that replaced it earns 65.7 bps, roughly 84× the custody rate. Huntington has already chosen to trade low-fee institutional processing for high-margin managed advice. Horizon executes the direction the bank is already moving. Honest caveat: part of that managed growth is Cadence and Veritex, not same-store.
