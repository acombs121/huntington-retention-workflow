Huntington Horizon Customer Demo Script

10-Minute Executive Presentation Walkthrough & Production Blueprint (v5.2)

What Problem Does This Solve?

Huntington's commercial book generates $4.5B in annual CRE and middle-market loan payoffs. Within 48–72 hours of asset closing, ~78% of net equity proceeds ($2M–$10M+) wire out to Wall Street competitors because commercial RMs lack the bandwidth to manually parse credit files (6–8 hrs/deal), wealth advisors are capped at 80 clients, and 1031 exchanges leak to third-party accommodators. Horizon provides dual-sided capacity leverage—monitoring 100% of the book, absorbing discovery in seconds, pre-staging wealth onboarding and servicing (expanding advisor capacity to 150 clients), and routing 1031 deposits into institutional escrow with zero net new headcount.
